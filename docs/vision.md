# Truman: vision

> Your reality is defined by the sum of all the little things you choose to pay attention to.

## Who it's for

People who want to keep up with the world without letting the news set their mood for them. They
read on a phone as often as a laptop, they come back once or twice a day, and they would rather
read ten stories that matter to them than scroll past a hundred that don't. Today that's the owner
of [truman.news](https://truman.news) and a handful of people signed in to it. Tomorrow it's anyone
who runs their own instance.

## What it's trying to be

A daily news feed that each reader shapes:

- **What it covers**: the tags they follow, rank or ban, and the values they hold, in the order
  they rank them.
- **How it feels**: a mood setting that keeps out stories above the sentiment they choose.
- **How it reads**: one presenter's voice, chosen by the reader, that rewrites each story around
  its actual claim and evidence, leaving the padding and the clickbait headline behind.

Truman does the reading so the reader doesn't have to. It fetches the feeds, works out what each
article claims and what that rests on, scores it against the values, and rewrites it. Every story
links back to its source.

## What it deliberately isn't

- **Not a social network.** No comments, likes, follower counts or shares as a ranking signal.
- **Not an engagement machine.** No infinite scroll tuned for time on site, and no notifications
  meant to bring people back.
- **Not a source of new facts.** A rewrite never adds a fact, figure, quote or source that isn't in
  the article.
- **Not a hosted service for strangers** yet. It's one personal instance, and others run their own.

## Who we learn from

- **[Ground News](https://ground.news)**: shows how outlets across the spectrum cover the same
  story, and where a reader's own diet is lopsided.
- **[Particle](https://particle.news)**: AI summaries of a story across many sources, with ways to
  read it at different depths.
- **[Artifact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artifact_(app))**: personalised news with AI
  rewrites of clickbait headlines; shut down in 2024, a lesson in how hard the business is.
- **[Positive News](https://www.positive.news) and [Good News Network](https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org)**:
  constructive journalism, proof that people want news that doesn't wear them down.
- **[Feedly](https://feedly.com) and [Readwise Reader](https://readwise.io/read)**: RSS readers
  that use AI to filter and prioritise what's worth reading.

## How to judge a proposal

1. Does a reader notice it within a day or two of using Truman, on their phone?
2. Does it give the reader more control over what they pay attention to, or less?
3. Does it keep every story faithful to its source?
4. Does it keep Truman cheap and simple to run on one small server?

## Next themes

1. **News that turns up every day.** The analysis pipeline died for two months without anyone
   noticing. Fresh articles should arrive every run, and a run that doesn't happen or produces
   nothing should be noticed the same day. Open: #175, #176, #163, #79, #73.
2. **Comfortable to read on a phone.** Readers check the news on their phone, and today the text is
   too small and they're logged out every week. Open: #52, #61, #45.
3. **Stories worth reading in full.** Many feeds carry only a teaser, so the rewrite has little to
   work with. Readers should get stories with enough substance to be worth the click, from sources
   they can see and choose. Open: none yet.
4. **Easy to run your own.** The README invites people to run their own instance. That should take
   one command and a few keys, with nothing left over from old deployments. Open: #123, #5.
